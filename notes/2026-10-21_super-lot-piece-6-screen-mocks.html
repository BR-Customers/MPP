What changes

  • The tab now also reads the LOT's audit log. CRT on/off, vendor returns, trim rework and the missed-inspection flag show here for the first time.
  • Every super-lot event that touches the LOT gets its own row: joined, result recorded or voided, deviation raised, released, removed, super-lot closed or cancelled.
  • Audit events that repeat a line already in the timeline (created, moved, status changed, die cast pieces added) are left out by the new flag on the event type, so nothing shows twice.
  • The Lot Detail report shows the same rows.

What changes

  • Every node that is or was in a super-lot carries a marker: amber while open, grey once released, closed or cancelled. A node can carry several.
