What changes

  • A new tab beside Scrap, built as its own embedded view like the Scrap and Count panels.
  • It's open only for Received or Received offsite LOTs. A pass-through part can be returned while it's still the received LOT, but not after MPP repacks it into a tray LOT.
  • Good or Hold LOTs can be marked. Returning the last pieces closes a Good LOT; a held LOT stays on Hold.
  • Cancel puts back exactly what was taken. It's refused once the parts are confirmed returned, or once the LOT has closed.
  • If the LOT is on an open super-lot, marking here also writes a Return result there, so the left-over count still adds up.
Check: marking needs a supervisor AD sign-in here. Should a Quality or receiving operator be able to mark with just their PIN?

What changes

  • Rework tile removed. A rework is now the supervisor override back into trim followed by the Trim OUT, so a typed count would count the pieces twice.
  • Return to vendor takes the pieces off the LOT when OK is pressed, the same way Scrap already does. There's no separate Apply step. It shows only for received LOTs.
  • A member at trim shows "At trim rework since …" with the supervisor who approved it. It can't be released until its Trim OUT is recorded.
  • After the Trim OUT, Quality records Accepted or Scrap for the reworked pieces as usual.
Your call (flagged by review): cancelling a return also voids its Return result, even after the super-lot is closed. The void is kept as history, not deleted. Leave it that way, or refuse a cancel once the super-lot has closed?
