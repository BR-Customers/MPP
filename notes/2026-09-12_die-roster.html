Built from Copy of Asset #'s for Dies.xlsx (170 die molds, 28 trim dies) and Aug 17_ 2026 Die Shots Report.html (343 part × die rows). A die appears once per part number it casts in the source report; those rows are collapsed here by clustering shot counts within a family / component / generation-letter bucket, then matched to the asset register on journal position and cam bank.
Cavity count is a lower bound — it counts distinct part numbers cast per shot, so a die with four identical cavities making one part number reads as 1. Regenerate: python reference/seed_data/parsers/build_die_roster.py. Analysis: notes/2026-09-12_die-asset-register-and-shot-report.md.
