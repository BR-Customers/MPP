What this mock is for

  • Layout and flow of the dedicated Quality view at /shop-floor/controlled-runs, drawn at a 1920 x 1080 terminal. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-controlled-run-super-lot-design.md.
  • Scenes 1 to 6 are the same page in different states. Use the scene buttons at the top.

Things to react to

  • One page, list on the left, the super-lot on the right. A scan anywhere on the page selects the super-lot it belongs to.
  • Results, Sign-off and History tabs are placeholders. They show where the later pieces would sit; nothing about them is designed yet.
  • Preview shows what cannot be added (already shipped, already in another super-lot, scrapped) instead of hiding it.
  • "Keep adding new production" is the open decision in the spec (section 9, item 3). Scene 1 shows it on; scene 6 shows the window ended.
  • Close is refused until every member is cleared and the window has ended, and the page says why.
  • The label is a drawing, not a ZPL render. Proportions are a 4 x 3 inch label; the real stock size needs confirming.
