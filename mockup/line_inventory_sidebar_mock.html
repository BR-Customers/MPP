M&A terminals — Line inventory sidebar

Mockup for review before build. One shared component, 320px wide and full height, docked right on Machining IN, Machining OUT, Assembly IN and both Assembly OUT screens. Rows are grouped by part and show the part description. Finished goods are never listed. Low rows are tinted orange with a bright orange border; there is no badge. Part descriptions and quantities below are illustrative.

01

Terminal with a finished good running

One line per part: description, available quantity, and a half-height button. Low = on hand below (quantity per finished good × the running finished good's Low-Inventory Horizon, e.g. 50); low rows are tinted orange and sort to the top, the rest alphabetical. +5,000 is one tap and uses the part's Box Quantity. + LOT is a PassThrough part with no Box Quantity and asks for a count. Made parts have no button. 12 rows fit with no scrolling at this height.
