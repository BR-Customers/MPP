What changes against today's popup

  • Position: the popup is pinned to the top half of the terminal, full width. Nothing in the popup needs the bottom half.
  • Reason: the dropdown becomes the Trim tile grid, ordered by most used on this line. The first 18 show without scrolling; "More reasons" expands to the rest. Tap = +1; the tile lights up and shows its count.
  • Quantity: the text box becomes the Trim line row (- / qty / + / remove). Tapping the number opens the project Numpad inside the popup, so the built-in keyboard never opens.
  • Remark: collapsed to one button. Tapping it expands a remark section with its keyboard in place of the reason tiles; Done (or tapping it again) collapses it back to the tiles. Part and Entered stay visible throughout, and nothing leaves the top half.
  • Several reasons in one entry, like Trim. Today the popup records one reason per open.
  • Over the on-hand count: capped as you tap, with a "Scrap capped" toast (Trim's behaviour), instead of the "N parts short - submit N instead?" second screen.
