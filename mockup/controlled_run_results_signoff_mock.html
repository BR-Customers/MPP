What this mock is for

  • The Results and Sign-off tabs of /shop-floor/controlled-runs (super-lot pieces 2 and 3), at a 1920 x 1080 terminal. Specs: 2026-10-12-controlled-run-completed-containers-design.md, 2026-10-12-controlled-run-results-and-signoff-design.md.

Results as an accordion

  • Header = the summary row (its own view, same columns as the Members tab). Body = the entry form (its own view), built like Record Scrap at Trim and Assembly: tap a tile to add 1, tap a quantity for the number pad.
  • Outcomes and scrap reasons are both tiles and both land in one Entered list, so there is one way to enter anything and one Save per member.
  • Accept the rest puts whatever is left over into Accepted in one tap.
  • Reason tiles are the ranked list for where the member was made, with More reasons for the full list, as on the line.

Things to react to

  • Left over is information, not a wall. A member can be released with pieces unaccounted for.
  • Scrap on a LOT happens immediately and shows as Applied. Scrap on a packed container is recorded and shows as Not yet applied until the repack piece exists.
  • "Quality sign-in, 24 min left" in the header is the open question about a longer sign-in for this screen. Signatures (scenes 5 and 6) ask for the password regardless.
  • Released replaces Cleared on the left-hand cards and the middle KPI.
