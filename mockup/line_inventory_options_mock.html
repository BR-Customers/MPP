Line Inventory — layout options at real scale

The RPY Cam Rocker Holder Set rolls up to 40 parts: 20 castings and 20 machined sub-assemblies (real Dev BOM). The four purchased parts are illustrative — Dev has no PassThrough parts until migration 0089 is applied there. Stock levels are random. Every panel is the real 320 px width and the same height, so what is cut off here is cut off on the terminal. Orange = below 50 sets; red = below 10 sets (a second setting, from option B).

OPTION A

One list, most urgent first, “+N more” footer

Sorted by how far each part is from its threshold (share of the 50-set horizon), not by raw count — raw count would put 2,600 bolts below 30 castings. The footer says what is hidden and whether any of it is low.

Assembly OUT, RPY line — all 44 parts in one list
  • Simplest to build (one sort change in SQL + a footer).
  • The top of the list is always the next problem.
  • On a 44-part line, a dozen low rows fill the panel; the purchased parts (the only ones with a button) can be pushed off it.
  • Every operator sees every part, including ones they cannot act on.
OPTION B

Category buttons that expand

Three buttons with part counts. A button turns orange when any part in it is below 50 sets, red below 10. Tap to expand: only the parts that need attention are listed underneath, with the check-in button on purchased parts.
