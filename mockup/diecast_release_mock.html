What changed

  • The count is the input, not the exception. “Basket closes at” was a read-out of press arithmetic; now it is the field, pre-filled. An operator who counts the basket types one number and is done.
  • “(if different)” is gone. It framed counting as overruling the system. D3 says the reading proposes and the count wins — two independent facts, allowed to disagree.
  • The press counter still works and is one field. A line that reads the counter types there and never touches the count. Nothing is taken away.
  • Three cells became one line of provenance. The 0 + 0 = 0 strip read as three broken tiles on a fresh basket.
  • Two paragraphs became one advisory in its own row with a fixed shrink, so it cannot ride over the buttons.

The layout defect it fixes

In the live dialog the yellow advisory overlapped the button row — a flex child at the default shrink:1 squeezed under content pressure.

Same class as the _CutoverScan panel fixes: trailing children left at default shrink while every sibling above was pinned.

Why it does not argue

  • The disagreement is stated, not challenged. No confirm step, no “are you sure”. The operator is the only person who can see the basket.
  • It reads “22 under the press figure” rather than “variance” — the gap belongs to the shift reconciliation screen, not to a basket release.
  • Scrap sits below the count because it is a separate additive fact: at the press the bad casting never entered the basket, so it is not subtracted from it.
  • Die-wide is deliberately absent. Release is one cavity's closing number; warm-up and quality-test shots belong to the shift entry (spec §11.2).
